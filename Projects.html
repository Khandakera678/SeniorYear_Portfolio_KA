Sprout Spring

Sprout spring is cozy educational 2d platformer game created to raise awareness about deforestation and biodiversity loss. The player is a little sprout navigating a through a landscape of chopped trees. The players goal is to collect water spirits and grow into a strong plant while evading a corrupt bear. Utilizing a cozy, comforting and stress free atmosphere to educate younger audiences about rainforest restoration.

Skills: HTML, CSS, Python,and Unity

Sky Shatterer: Forest

This 2D arcade-style space shooter challenges players to survive and defeat waves of hostile alien spacecraft within a dense jungle environment. Through developing this project, I learned how to pogram a 2d responsive player and use conditional logic, loops.

Skills: HTML, CSS, and Unity
